    On the real page

    How this concept maps onto the login page we ship.

    • The card opens on a first step: Log in or Sign up. Log in asks for the email first, then the password. Sign up takes your name and email, then a password, then confirms.
    • Sign up keeps today's rules: at least 10 characters, @thebackroomop.com addresses only, and new accounts start as Viewer until an admin assigns a role.
    • The app has no password reset today, so the password step points people to an admin.
    • Microsoft and quick sign-in show only when they're turned on, same as today. Quick sign-in becomes one dropdown instead of a button per account.
    • The room is decoration. Screen readers skip it, and only the day timeline and pause button take clicks. With reduced motion turned on, Cris stays still.
    • The scene pauses while the tab is in the background. On phones the room becomes a banner above the card.
    • Account emails here are examples. The real dropdown lists the accounts in QUICK_LOGIN_EMAILS that exist and are enabled.
