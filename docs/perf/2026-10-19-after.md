## Actions (writers), slowest p95 first

Times in ms. A `server action · …` row is the server action's POST alone (request to response end, from the browser's network timing). The targets judge an action on it only when it has at least 20 fully timed rows; with fewer, on the action's whole step instead (an upper bound), and the Targets table says which basis and how many rows were timed. Every other row is the whole step in the browser: for a change, from the confirming click to the success toast (the action's round trip and the toast's render, before the page refresh, which starts after the toast); for an `open …` row, the navigation until the page's heading shows (its streamed content revealed). A skipped step (no spare free, empty approval queue, decision cap reached) has no time and is not an error. *Aborted after success* counts server-action responses the browser cut off (net::ERR_ABORTED) after their step had already succeeded: not errors, and not timed; *First byte* is the server's first byte for those of them the browser had timed (count · p50 · p95), a lower bound on their server time. Responses cut off by the hard stop are counted in neither.
